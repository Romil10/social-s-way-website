KaarigarDesignWeek.

Fifteen studios from across India bring one piece each to a former printing hall in south Delhi, for four days of objects made slowly, shown plainly and explained by the people who made them.

01 / Exhibition

Material memory

The 2027 edition asks each studio to show a single piece that carries the knowledge of a material, a place and a pair of hands.

A kaarigar is the person who knows how a thing is made. This year the hall is arranged as a quiet grid of fifteen equal rooms, so that a tray of inlaid zinc from Bidar stands at the same scale as a daybed of reclaimed teak, and every visitor can walk the whole show in under an hour.

Curation

One studio, one piece, one room

Each maker is given a three metre square booth with two white walls and a low plinth. Nothing is crowded, and every label is written by the studio itself.

Programme

Talks at the Forum, work at the booths

Conversations take place on the raised Forum by the entrance. Demonstrations and small workshops happen inside the booths, beside the work they explain.

Acquisition

Pieces can be commissioned

Every piece on show is available to order or commission directly from its studio. Prices are listed openly on the plan, and the fair takes no commission from makers.
