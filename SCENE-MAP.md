## Brand voice

## World description (one camera, one light)
A sunlit atelier room at golden hour. Warm cream plaster walls (background plate).
A long walnut worktable seen straight-on at eye level (midground plate), holding
papers, a brass lamp, ceramic vessels. Tall walnut-and-frosted-glass atelier doors
(foreground L/R) stand open at the edges of frame. On the table centre, the hero
object: a portfolio case in caramel leather with a linen spine (the agency's craft
object). Light direction: warm key from frame left, soft fill from right. Horizon /
sight-line: table surface at 62% of frame height. Palette: cream #F4EDE3, walnut
#5C4633, caramel #B07D4F, ink #1E1A16, accent brass #A67C2E.
