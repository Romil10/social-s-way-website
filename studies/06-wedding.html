The House

A small studio that plans fewer weddings, more slowly.

We take a limited number of celebrations each season so that the people who meet you in the first week are the same people standing beside the mandap on the last day. Our work begins with how your families gather, eat and laugh, and only then turns to flowers, fabric and light.

Everything we design is drawn first on paper, costed honestly, and made with craftspeople we have known for years: florists in Udaipur, tent makers in Jodhpur, and bakers and bands along the Goan coast.

i.

Listening before designing

An opening conversation of two unhurried hours, with both families if you wish, to understand rituals, elders, food and the music that must be played.

ii.

One palette, many rooms

Each event is its own room with its own mood, yet a single colour story runs from the haldi yellow of the first morning to the reception candlelight.

iii.

Calm on the day

Run sheets written to the minute, a dedicated family host, and a quiet room kept ready for grandparents who need a rest between ceremonies.

      Udaipur, by the lake

      Palace courtyards that hold six hundred guests, lakeside ghats for the pheras, and rooftop terraces for an evening of ghazals. We know which walls glow at five and which breezes arrive at nine.

      October to March

      The Konkan coast

      Goa, by the sea

      Old Portuguese villas with oyster-shell windows, gardens under rain trees, and beaches where the vows can be timed to the last of the sun. Barefoot where you like, formal where it matters.

      Best months
      November to February
      Guests
      Forty to three hundred and fifty
      Suits

      How we work

      From the first letter to the last dance.

      Week one

      i.Conversation

      We meet, in person or by call, to hear your story, your families and the rituals that matter. You leave with a written note of everything we heard.

      Weeks two to six

      ii.Design

      A bound design book with palettes, florals, drawings of each event, a venue shortlist and an honest budget broken down line by line.

      Months two to ten

      iii.Making

      Vendors contracted, samples approved, guest travel and rooms arranged, and a full rehearsal of every ceremony with the pandit or officiant.

      The days themselves

      iv.Hosting

      We run every hour on a shared run sheet, so that you and your parents are guests at your own wedding and never the ones holding a clipboard.
