04  /  Method

From garden row to glass bottle.

We work in small batches at our workshop in Thrissur, close to the farms that grow for us, so that we can trace each lot of plant material back to the field it came from.

i.

Grown and gathered

Partner farms in Idukki, Wayanad and Palakkad grow our plants, and harvest them by hand when each is at its best: leaves in the cool of the morning, rhizomes when the foliage has yellowed.

ii.

Prepared slowly

Leaves and flowers are extracted gently into water and glycerin. Turmeric is extracted with carbon dioxide for a cleaner, lower-staining result, and vetiver root is steam-distilled for its oil.

iii.

Formulated simply

Each formula is built around one or two botanicals at considered levels, with a short list of supporting ingredients. We would rather use fewer plants well than many plants for the sake of a long list.

iv.

Checked before release

Every batch is tested for stability and microbial safety before it leaves the workshop, and carries a batch code that links it to its plant lots and its date of making.

05  /  Our word

What we will say, and what we never will.

Ayurveda is a living tradition, and it deserves better than exaggeration. We describe our products as cosmetics, because that is what they are.

We will tell you

  • How a product feels on the skin and how it helps skin look.
  • The accepted botanical name of every plant, and the part we use.
  • Every ingredient, in order, including preservatives.
  • When a formula contains fragrance, and where that fragrance comes from.
  • Where a plant was grown, and when it was harvested.

We will not claim

  • That any product treats, heals or cures a skin condition.
  • That a plant is safe for everyone simply because it is natural.
  • Results in a set number of days, or percentages we cannot show you.
  • That our routine replaces advice from a doctor or dermatologist.
  • Ancient secrets. Everything we use is written down on this page.
Patch test first, every time.

Apply a little of any new product to the inside of your forearm and leave it for 48 hours before using it on your face. If your skin becomes red or itchy, rinse it off and stop using it. If you have a skin condition, please speak to a dermatologist before starting a new routine.
