01 The studio

A plan drawn around light, water and quiet.

We rebuilt a 1970s bungalow as one long room of lime plaster and dark oak, with six chairs along the north window so every cut happens in even daylight.

The wash bay sits behind a stone wall where the noise of the street falls away. Two spa rooms open onto a small courtyard with a frangipani tree, and a thin line of oud burns at the door each morning. Hover or tap a room to find it on the plan.

    03 Spa rituals

    The head ritual, in four slow movements.

    1. i.

      Breath and oil

      We warm sesame oil scented with a trace of oud, and begin with three slow breaths while it rests on the crown.

    2. ii.

      Pressure along the scalp

      Long, even strokes follow the lines of the skull, loosening the tension that gathers from screens and long commutes.

    3. iii.

      Neck and shoulders

      The work moves down into the shoulders with warm linen, so the whole upper body settles before the wash.

    4. iv.

      Rinse and tea

      A gentle wash at the basin, a cool towel, and a cup of tulsi tea in the courtyard before you step back out.

    Before colour

    If you have not coloured with us before, we ask for a small patch test 48 hours ahead. It takes five minutes and can be done on any visit.

    You can move or cancel without charge until 12 hours before. After that, we keep a small fee of ₹500 for the chair.
