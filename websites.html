VHow we work

Five steps, no surprises.

The same order for every level. What changes is how long each step takes, never whether it happens.

  1. i

    Discovery

    A conversation about your business, your customers and the ones you want more of.

  2. ii

    Direction

    The structure of the site drawn out and agreed, along with any branding directions.

  3. iii

    Design

    The pages take shape in your brand, reviewed with you as they come together.

  4. iv

    Build

    Built clean and fast, then tested on the phones and networks your customers use.

  5. v

    Handover

    Launched, walked through and handed over complete: files, accounts and passwords, all yours.
